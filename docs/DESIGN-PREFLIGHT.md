# Openglaze design preflight

This receipt ties the title, the stone and the purpose together before the mark lands. It follows the `kyanite-product-design-preflight` skill. A choice this file can't back is marked as an assumption.

## 1. Purpose

OpenGlaze is a free, open-source ceramic glaze calculator, recipe manager and UMF analyser for potters.

- Outcome for the user: Potters predict and record glaze results from chemistry, without proprietary software.

## 2. Title, stone and purpose

| Layer | Content | Evidence class |

## 3. Rejected metaphors

- UMF unity triangle in three stones (C2, retired by the owner on 2026-11-10).
- A red diamond placeholder or neon slab (earlier live marks).
- A clean, uniform glaze. The owner asked for weird, layered shino.

## 4. Material reference

Shino: a thick feldspathic glaze that crawls, pinholes and carbon-traps. Tenmoku: an iron glaze that breaks rust-gold to hare's-fur. Wood ash: olive-amber runs. The body is speckled buff stoneware with an unglazed foot.

The landed render is `branding/openglaze.png`: A stoneware test tile dipped diagonally: crawled white shino with wood-ash runs on one side, tenmoku over shino with hare's-fur on the other, and an intermixed overlap band.

## 5. Family law and form

- The icon must read as an icon: one compact, bold silhouette that holds at 32 px and 16 px. No scene, pedestal, text, rays or glow.
- The real mineral shows in broad regions.
- Family palette: the crystal material itself, turquoise `#00FFE6` edges, magenta `#FF1A6E` and yellow `#FFD11A` catches, white speculars, dark inner planes, transparent background.
- Form: One upright L-shaped test tile with a short foot, split-dipped on the diagonal.
- Approval: the owner's eye-gate (2026-11-10). A concept SVG is never the icon; the mark is a pipeline render.

## 6. Typography

- Wordmark: **OPENGLAZE** in Chakra Petch Bold 700, uppercase, unmodified glyphs, native kerning.
